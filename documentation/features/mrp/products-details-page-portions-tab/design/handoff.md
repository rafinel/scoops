# Accompaniment Portions tab design handoff

The saved PNGs are the implementation references. Node IDs are provenance only. Implementation,
Builders and the Visual Reviewer use this handoff and the images and must not reopen Pencil. All
references were exported from `design/onoreo.pen` at scale 1 on 2026-10-20 and visually inspected.
Dialog exports include 75 px of transparent shadow padding on every side, so their PNGs are larger
than the measured node size.

Approved Issue #67 frames: `Nlypf`, `K94D7U`, `i4m0t`, `vrB3x`, reusing `DyrWo`, `q2gZuS` and
`C8la0H` (their full measurements are in
[global-accompaniments/design/handoff.md](../../global-accompaniments/design/handoff.md)). During
clarification (Q4, Q6) `vrB3x` gained a scrollable picker and eleven frames were added. In revision 2
(Q19/Q20) `Nlypf`, `i4m0t`, `Ukgbr` and `fAy9D` gained `Tornar global`, `K94D7U` gained `Tornar individual`
(hidden again in `o0dUMY` per Q23), and two dialogs were added (`V4tfiT`, `z6Ur3D`). In revision 3
(Q24) the `Disponível para todas as porções` switch row was removed from `vrB3x`, `A4lMzz`, `hl9fU`,
`jW65M` and `V4tfiT`, frame `Ks7Gs` was deleted, and two Porções-tab Edit dialogs were added
(`s30kJA`, `JyW9f`). In revision 4 (Q25) `DyrWo` and `y7dXvq` (Portion side) lost the locked switch and their notice gained
"Para deixar de ser global, use Remover na tabela."; these exports supersede the global-accompaniments
bundle's `DyrWo`/`y7dXvq` (that bundle is historical). The switch remains only for non-global items
(`iSdux`, `DT6Vc` in that bundle).
