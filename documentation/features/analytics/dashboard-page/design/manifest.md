## Responsive and layout contract

- Desktop keeps the four KPI cards in one row and uses the chart/stock and product/channel two-column pairings shown in `R1X3j`.
- Below the desktop breakpoint, replace the direct period pills with one labeled selector, keep the KPI cards in a 2×2 grid, and order Stock now before Sales evolution, followed by Products and Channels.
- At 320px, headings, values, toggles, links and status copy must wrap or stack without overlap, clipping or horizontal document scrolling. The corrected `cmnYV` arrangement is authoritative.
- Data density may reduce chart tick labels at narrow widths, but buckets and exact values remain available through the table.
- Initial sales loading reserves the final desktop or narrow geometry for all four KPIs, cancellation, evolution chart, products and channels; initial stock loading independently reserves the stock card. Header and period controls remain visible. Each source exposes one loading status, hides decorative skeleton pieces from assistive technology and disables shimmer under reduced motion. Cached refreshes retain real content; one source resolving or failing must not erase or re-skeletonize the other.
